# Page 5 — Support: Contact Us

**Status:** draft, for review.
**Template:** standard (Page 0).
**URL:** `support/contact`.
**Why this is a page (not just the on-index "Talk to us" block):** two reasons — (a) it's the single most-linked-to destination in the site (every Orientation page cross-links "→ Contact us"), so it needs a stable, predictable URL rather than an anchor mid-index; (b) local-SEO: search engines treat a dedicated contact page as a known signal slot and crawl it for org identity/NAP in a way a footer or an on-index block doesn't. The on-index "Talk to us" block and this page carry the **same core info in different display formats** — this is the one deliberate content echo, justified by the linkability + SEO roles.
**Entry points:** the header "Contact Us" CTA button (present on every standard page); "→ Contact us" cross-links from Orientation pages; footer.
**Job of this page:** make reaching a human as low-effort and unintimidating as possible, and set honest expectations about what contact with Sands is (and isn't).

**Uses L2 sub-template blocks:** L1 (intro), L3 (prose/reassurance), L7 (back to Support), L8 (footer). No jump-to nav (single idea), no What's On cross-link.

---

## A. Page intro
- Short, warm opening. This is the first thing three different Orientation journeys land on, so it should feel like being met, not like a form. State plainly what happens when you get in touch.

## B. How to reach us (primary content)
- **Phone** — the callback mobile. Set expectations honestly (this is the whole point of the page): it's not a staffed hotline; leave a message and a volunteer returns it, typically within 24 hrs. Nothing here implies urgency — the tone is "we'll be here," not "call now."
- **Email** — for those who'd rather write than talk.
- **"A family member or friend can make the first call for you"** — carried from V0, important: lowers the effort barrier for someone who can't face phoning themselves.
- Who answers: volunteers who are bereaved parents, not counsellors — we listen and share, we don't clinically advise. (Sets honest expectations, per the copy-tone rule of saying what Sands is *not*.)

## C. What contact with Sands is / isn't
- Brief, plain restatement: peer support from people who've been there; not counselling, not crisis response. Manages expectations so no one arrives expecting the wrong thing.

## D. (Deliberately NOT here) crisis numbers
- The health/crisis 24-hr lines do **not** appear on this page — they live on the Support index "Talk to us" block only. Reasoning (chat + SEO review): they aren't Sands' own NAP, so duplicating them here adds no SEO value and creates a second place to maintain them. If a visitor needs immediate crisis help, the Support index block carries it; this page is specifically Sands' own contact channel.
- **Flag:** confirm this is comfortable — the counter-argument is that someone in acute distress landing directly on `support/contact` won't see crisis numbers. If that risk outweighs the tidiness, the crisis list moves here too. Dan's call.
