# Page 4 — Support Index

**Status:** draft, for review. **Supersedes the earlier A/B/C-grouping version** — that draft fanned Support out into ~10 thin sub-pages; this version collapses almost all of it onto the index as on-page content blocks, per the depth-ceiling review. Only 3 real sub-pages remain (contact, brochures full list, library & books full list).
**Template:** standard (Page 0).
**URL:** `support/index` — nav label "Support."
**Job of this page:** the site's "ways we can help" page. Most support content lives *here*, on the page, as blocks — not scattered across sub-pages. Sub-pages exist only where a full list is genuinely too long to sit inline (brochures, library) or where a stable cross-link target + SEO anchor is warranted (contact). Per content schedule: warm, second-person, present-tense, **no dates as instances** — anything time-bound is stated as a standing rule and cross-links to What's On.

**Consolidation principle (chat decision):** a content type only earns its own page if (a) it has more than ~1 screen of content, or (b) it's a frequent cross-link target needing a stable URL. Everything failing both tests became an on-index block.

## B. Talk to us
*The active, "reach a human now" block. This is also the content that the many "→ Contact us" cross-links elsewhere point at — though those may point at the dedicated `support/contact` page instead (see that brief); this block and that page carry the same core info in different display formats.*

- **Sands' own channel first, always:** phone (callback mobile — set expectation: message returned within 24 hrs, a family member or friend can make the first call if you can't), email. Volunteers are bereaved parents, not counsellors.
- **Sub-element — "other options to call":** health & crisis 24-hr lines, framed as *immediate help beyond Sands*, never visually above Sands' own line. Labelled, not bare numbers:
  - Healthline (24 hr): 0800 611 116
  - Lifeline (24 hr): 0800 543 354
  - Crisis Team, Auckland City (CATT): 0800 800 717
  - Plunketline: 0800 933 922
  - Relationship Services: 0800 735 283
- Sensitivity note (content schedule): this touches distress — tone matters, not just a number dump.
- Treatment: prose/reassurance for the Sands channel; the crisis lines as a distinct scannable list beneath (label + number), visually secondary to the Sands channel above.
- **Note:** crisis numbers live *here* (and on the index only) — deliberately NOT duplicated onto `support/contact` (see that brief for the reasoning; they aren't Sands' own NAP and duplicating them adds nothing).

## C. Support group meetings
*Two meeting modes as sub-elements of one block — face-to-face and online. Both stated as standing patterns, not dated instances.*

- **Face-to-face monthly meetings:** what to expect walking in; adults only; you needn't speak; facilitated by a volunteer. Standing rule: first Thursday monthly, Feb–Dec (no January), 7:15–8:45pm, Garlick Room, Mount Albert Community & Leisure Centre, 773 New North Rd. → cross-link to What's On for the next actual date.
- **Online support meetings:** Sands NZ closed Facebook groups (Bereaved Whānau / Bereaved Dads / Bereaved Grandparents / Whānau Info). What they are, how to join. External links to the FB groups.
- Treatment: two labelled sub-sections within one block. The standing meeting schedule wants a quiet standout (callout, not buried in prose) so it's scannable.

## Decision log
- **Collapsed ~10 sub-pages to 3.** Depth-ceiling review found 5+ of the original Support children were under one screen of content — proliferation without payoff. Consolidation rule applied: own page only if >1 screen OR a frequent cross-link target.
- **Crisis numbers stay on-index only, not duplicated to contact page** — they aren't Sands' NAP, so duplicating them carries no SEO benefit and adds maintenance surface.
- **Events overview on-index, dates stay in What's On** — no events sub-page; avoids duplicating dated content and the staleness trap.
- **Online-resources folded inline** — assessed by volume, ~8 annotated links don't warrant a page.
