When your baby dies

⚠ DRAFT copy — this page carries the project's highest-priority copy review. Every word below is placeholder pending review by both a bereaved mother and a bereaved father. Do not ship as-is.

Your baby has died. We are so sorry. Whatever has happened, and however early, you are not alone — and there is nothing you have to decide or do right now.

The next days can feel impossible to think through. You may feel one way today and another tomorrow; both are normal. When you are ready, we're here — people who have been where you are.

There is no hurry

There are very few decisions that truly cannot wait. Other people may offer to make them for you — but these days are also a chance to be with your baby and to build memories, if you want to. You can take whatever time you need.

What Sands offers costs you nothing and asks nothing back. The gentlest first: you can simply read. When you want a little more, you can email. When you're ready to talk, you can call, or come to a meeting.

Mothers

DRAFT — needs review You carried this baby, and your grief is your own. However your baby died, and whatever anyone else expects of you, your feelings are yours to have. We won't rush you and we won't measure your loss.

Fathers & partners

DRAFT — needs review Nothing could prepare you for this. Your grief matters just as much, even if the world keeps asking how she is and not how you are. You are a parent here, fully — not a supporter on the sidelines.

DRAFT — needs review If you are facing this without a partner beside you, you are just as welcome. Nothing here assumes a couple. You do not need anyone's permission, and you do not need a reason, to reach out.

Help you can get in the hospital

Sands support packs — information pamphlets, inkless kits and memory booklets — are offered through Auckland City, North Shore and Waitākere hospitals, through independent LMCs, or directly from us. These can be an important part of making memories when there is often so little time.
