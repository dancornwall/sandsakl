Support our work

Sands Auckland Central is a voluntary, non-profit group run by bereaved parents. The Ministry of Health funds our support packs nationally, but we receive no other government funding.

Donations pay for everything else we do: support meetings, resources for bereaved families, Baby Loss Awareness Week events, visits to hospitals to deliver support items and liaise with staff, and training and self-care for our volunteers. Any gesture of support is greatly appreciated. We are a registered charity — CC30352.
