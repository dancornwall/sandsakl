Baby Loss Awareness Week

9–15 October, every year. A time for parents and whānau around New Zealand to come together and remember the lives of their babies who have died — any gestation, any length of life, however they died. It is also when we raise awareness in the wider community. What remembrance means at Sands.

Annual Remembrance Day

A Sands Auckland service to remember our babies together. confirm this year's date & venue Families we haven't seen for a while are welcome, and so are newly bereaved parents.

Global Wave of Light

15 October, 7:00 pm. On International Pregnancy and Infant Loss Remembrance Day, families light a candle to burn for one hour, in memory of all the babies who lit up our lives for such a short time. Aotearoa begins the wave, setting off a continuous line of light around the globe. You are warmly welcome to join from home, or at one of these events:

Light Up Aotearoa

Each year Sands NZ arranges for landmarks across the country to be lit in blue, pink and purple for Baby Loss Awareness Week. Participating landmarks have included:

  • Auckland — Holy Trinity Cathedral, Mission Bay Fountain, Aotea Square, Eden Park, Civic Centre
  • Ashburton — Clock Tower
  • Blenheim — War Memorial Clock Tower, Seymour Square Fountain
  • Christchurch — Art Gallery, Botanical Gardens, New Brighton Pier, Bridge of Remembrance
  • Dunedin — St Paul's Cathedral, Otago Museum, Toitū Settlers Museum
  • Invercargill — Queens Park Fountain, Feldwick Gates, Band Rotunda
  • Napier — Tom Parker Fountain
  • New Plymouth — Clock Tower
  • Palmerston North — Clock Tower
  • Taupō — Great Lake Centre
  • Tauranga — Wharf St Lights
  • Wellington — Cable Cars, Michael Fowler Centre, Carter Fountain
  • Whanganui — Royal Opera House, War Memorial
  • Whangārei — Victoria Canopy Bridge

A live map of lighting times is published each year on the Sands NZ Facebook page.

Sands NZ National Conference

Our national conference is held every two years — a time to gather, to remember our babies, and to look ahead. It welcomes bereaved parents and families, health professionals, and anyone with an interest in pregnancy, baby and infant loss. The most recent was held in Wellington in June 2025.

Details of the next biennial conference will be posted when available. sandsnationalconference.org.nz
