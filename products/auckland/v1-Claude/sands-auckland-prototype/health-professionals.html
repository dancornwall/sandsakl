Information for health professionals

For midwives, nurses, LMCs, social workers and anyone caring for a family after the death of a baby.

We are available by phone or email to offer information at any time during your care of a family — at diagnosis, during pregnancy, at the death, or through a subsequent pregnancy.

We also promote self-care for health and caring professionals. If this work is affecting you, you can call us too.

Support packs and pamphlets

PDF copies of all of the pamphlets provided in the Sands support pack are available from Sands New Zealand: sands.org.nz/help-pamphlets.html

The pack contains six pamphlets: The Next Few Days, Your Baby's Funeral, Fathers Grieve Too, When a Baby Brother or Sister Dies, The Loss of a Grandchild, and Transporting Your Baby.
