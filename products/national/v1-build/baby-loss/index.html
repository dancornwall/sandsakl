You are not alone

Wherever you are in your grief, you are welcome here. Whether your baby died recently or many years ago, during pregnancy, around birth or in infancy, Sands New Zealand is here to support you. We are a network of bereaved parents and whānau supporting others across Aotearoa.

You may not know what you need right now, and that is okay. The options below offer a few places to begin. Choose whichever feels most relevant to you.

My pregnancy ended for medical reasons

If your pregnancy ended following a medical diagnosis, you may be grieving your baby while carrying many complex emotions. Sands offers compassionate, non-judgemental support and a place where your baby and your experience will be acknowledged.

I'm pregnant after loss

Someone I love has lost a baby

About Sands

Sands New Zealand is a parent-run network. All our volunteers are also bereaved parents. We are not counsellors, but we do offer something important: the chance to share experiences, to talk, and to be listened to by people who have been there.

More about us

Sands offers empathy and understanding. We are not counsellors but do importantly offer an opportunity to share experiences, talk and listen.

Sands runs on grants and donations. If you're able to, you can help us keep this support going.
