Accessibility

⚠ DRAFT — newly written; please review before launch.

We want this site to be usable by everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, or other assistive technology. It matters to us that reaching support is never made harder by the way a page is built.

What we aim for

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA. In practice that means:

  • text and controls that meet AA colour-contrast levels;
  • every interactive element reachable and usable by keyboard, with a visible focus indicator;
  • the site working down to small screens and when text is enlarged;
  • meaningful text alternatives for images;
  • respecting a reduced-motion preference where any motion is used.

Tell us if something isn't working

If any part of this site is difficult to use, please let us know so we can put it right. Email info@sands.org.nz or call Find a local group.
