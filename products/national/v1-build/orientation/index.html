You are not alone

Whatever has happened, whatever the gestation of your baby, and however long ago — you are welcome here. Sands New Zealand is a network of bereaved parents supporting other families across Aotearoa after the death of a baby.

Below are a few different places to start. Read whichever one feels closest to where you are — there's no wrong door, and you're welcome to look at more than one.

About Sands

Sands New Zealand is a parent-run network. All our volunteers are also bereaved parents. We are not counsellors, but we do offer something important: the chance to share experiences, to talk, and to be listened to by people who have been there.

More about us

Sands offers empathy and understanding. We are not counsellors but do importantly offer an opportunity to share experiences, talk and listen.

Sands runs on grants and donations. If you're able to, you can help us keep this support going. Support our work
