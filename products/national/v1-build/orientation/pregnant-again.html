I'm pregnant again

⚠ DRAFT copy — orientation copy needs bereaved-parent review before it ships.

Pregnancy after loss is not the same as pregnancy. Alongside any hope, there is often fear — and the two can sit together in a way that is hard to explain to people who haven't been through it. However you are feeling, it makes sense here.

Fear and hope, at the same time

Milestones that once felt joyful can now feel fraught. You may find yourself holding your breath through scans and appointments, or holding back from getting attached. You may feel disloyal to the baby who died, or guilty for feeling happy. None of this means anything is wrong with you.

You're welcome to reach out

You are welcome to contact us and to come to our meetings, wherever you are in a subsequent pregnancy. Talking with others who have carried this particular mix of fear and hope can help.
