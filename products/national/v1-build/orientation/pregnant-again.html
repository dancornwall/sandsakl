I'm pregnant after loss

Being pregnant after the death of a baby can feel very different from pregnancy before loss. Hope and love may sit alongside fear, anxiety and grief. These feelings can change from one day or moment to the next, and there is no right way to experience this pregnancy.

The sense of safety or certainty you once had may be gone. You know that pregnancy does not always end as hoped, and it can be difficult to trust that things will be different this time.

Hope and fear can sit together

Scans, appointments and pregnancy milestones may bring reassurance, anxiety or both. You may find yourself holding your breath, checking for signs that your baby is well, or feeling unable to look too far ahead.

You may feel hopeful and excited, or you may be afraid to hope or become attached. You might also feel guilty about experiencing happiness, or worry that welcoming another baby could somehow be disloyal to the baby who died.

Whatever you feel, loving and hoping for this baby does not lessen your love for the baby you lost. This baby is not a replacement, and your grief does not take away from the love you have for this pregnancy.

Care during this pregnancy

Let your midwife, doctor or maternity team know about your previous loss and any fears you are carrying. You can ask what additional care or monitoring may be appropriate and talk with them about what would help you feel informed and supported.

It may be helpful to:

  • discuss a care plan early in your pregnancy
  • write down questions before appointments
  • ask for clear explanations about scans and results
  • take a trusted support person with you
  • let your healthcare team know when anxiety feels difficult to manage

Your healthcare team can also help you access professional support if anxiety is affecting your wellbeing or daily life.

Sands is here

You are welcome to contact Sands at any stage of a pregnancy after loss. Connecting with bereaved parents who understand this particular mixture of hope, fear and grief can help you feel less alone.

We have a monthly online support meeting for Pregnancy After Loss that you are welcome to join. [Link to Pregnancy After Loss meeting]

We are here to listen and walk alongside you, one day at a time.
