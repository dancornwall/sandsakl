Baby Loss Awareness Week

9–15 October, every year — ending with International Pregnancy and Infant Loss Remembrance Day on 15 October.

A time for parents, families and whānau across Aotearoa to come together and remember the lives of their babies who have died — any gestation, any length of life, however they died. It is also when Sands raises awareness in the wider community. Local groups often hold their own services; national programmes sit alongside them.

Global Wave of Light

15 October, 7:00 pm. Families light a candle for one hour in memory of the babies who lit up our lives for such a short time. Aotearoa begins the wave, setting off a continuous line of light around the globe.

Sands NZ online national service

Sands NZ hosts an online service each year (hosting often rotates between regions). Join from about 6:50 pm for a 7 pm start.

Light Up Aotearoa

Each year for Baby Loss Awareness Week, landmarks across the country are lit mawhero (pink), kikorangi (blue) and poroporo (purple) to remember our babies and acknowledge whānau who have suffered loss.

Participating places change year to year — from cathedrals and clock towers to bridges, galleries and civic landmarks nationwide.

Sands NZ National Conference

Every two years Sands New Zealand hosts a national conference — days to gather, remember our babies, learn, and look ahead. It welcomes bereaved parents and families, health professionals, and anyone with an interest in pregnancy, baby and infant loss. Hosting rotates among area groups.
