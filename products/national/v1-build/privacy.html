Privacy

Sands New Zealand respects your privacy. Grief is deeply personal, and the trust families place in us matters more than anything on this page.

Information you give us

When you contact us — by phone, email, or message — we use your details only to respond to you and to provide what you've asked for. We do not sell or share your information, and we keep it no longer than we need to.

This website

This site does not use tracking or advertising cookies. Links out to other organisations (for example Whetūrangitia or crisis lines) take you to sites with their own privacy policies.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or a request to see or remove information we hold about you, can go to info@sands.org.nz or Find a local group.
