The NZ Board

Sands New Zealand is governed by a volunteer board of bereaved parents. They support the network of local groups, national programmes, and the resources families and professionals rely on.

Details below are taken from the live sands.org.nz board page. Confirm roster is current before launch — the old page still headed 2019–2021 in places.

Chairperson
