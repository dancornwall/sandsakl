For health professionals

Information for midwives, nurses, LMCs and other professionals caring for a family whose baby has died, or may die. If you're caring for a family right now, you can reach us any time on Find a local group or info@sands.org.nz.

Ordering support packs & materials

Sands support packs contain information pamphlets, inkless kits and memory booklets for families in your care. The packs are the Ministry of Health–funded item, provided through Auckland City, North Shore and Waitākere hospitals; we can also supply them directly or via independent LMCs.

All the pamphlets are free to download and share:

  • Support resources — pamphlets, support-pack information and the Certificate of Life
  • Sands NZ help pamphlets — the national pamphlet library

Contact us to arrange packs for your unit.

Referring a family — when to contact Sands

You're welcome to put a family in touch with us, or to call us for information, at any point in your care — for example:

  • at diagnosis
  • during pregnancy
  • at the time of death
  • in a subsequent pregnancy

Our volunteers are bereaved parents offering peer support. We are not counsellors and not a crisis service — we listen, and we share what helped us.

Self-care for professionals

Caring for bereaved families takes something out of you, too. We promote self-care for health and caring professionals, and we're available by phone or email to talk things through — you can call us on your own behalf, not only a family's.

Training

Since 2021, Sands NZ and Vicki Culling Associates have provided free training to midwifery, nursing and social work students — online content plus discussion webinars with bereaved parents, held about three times a year. Recent topics have included:

  • Early Loss
  • Perinatal Hospice
  • Ending a Wanted Pregnancy
  • Self-Care for Professionals Working with Baby Loss

Information and registrations are available through Vicki Culling Associates and the Sands NZ Facebook page — spaces are limited. Volunteers from Sands Auckland are also available to give talks about Sands and our own experiences. Dated sessions appear on What's On.
