Support our work

Sands New Zealand is a voluntary, non-profit group run by bereaved parents. The Ministry of Health funds our support packs nationally, but we receive no other government funding.

Donations pay for everything else we do: support meetings, resources for bereaved families, Baby Loss Awareness Week events, visits to hospitals to deliver support items and liaise with staff, and training and self-care for our volunteers. Any gesture of support is greatly appreciated. We are a registered charity — charity number tbc.

Donate via Givealittle

Give securely online to Sands New Zealand — a simple way to make a one-off or regular gift. No matter how big or small, every bit helps provide services to families throughout Aotearoa.

Grants & sponsorship

We welcome partnership with funders and businesses who want to support families after the death of a baby. Get in touch to talk about what partnership could look like.
