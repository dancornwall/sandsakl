Support our work

Sands New Zealand is a volunteer-led, non-profit organisation supporting bereaved parents and whānau throughout Aotearoa.

Some of our national bereavement resources are supported through the public health system, but Sands receives no operational government funding. Our wider work is made possible through grants, fundraising, donations and the generosity of our volunteers.

Your support helps us provide:

  • compassionate support for bereaved parents and whānau
  • meetings and opportunities for connection
  • practical information and memory-making resources
  • support items for hospitals and medical facilities
  • education and awareness activities
  • training and wellbeing support for our volunteers

Every contribution makes a difference. Whether you donate, fundraise, volunteer your time or help raise awareness, you are helping ensure bereaved whānau can find support when they need it.

Other ways to help

Become a volunteer

Share your time and skills to help Sands support bereaved whānau across Aotearoa. Whether you can help regularly or occasionally, there may be a role for you.

About volunteering

Grants and sponsorship

We welcome support from funders and businesses who share our commitment to bereaved parents and whānau. Get in touch to discuss how we could work together.

Fundraising events

From time to time, Sands groups hold fundraising events to support their work with bereaved whānau. Visit What’s On or your local groups’ social media pages to find upcoming events and ways to take part.

Donate items to a local group

Sands groups welcome handmade and practical items, such as blankets, baby clothing and memory boxes. Needs vary between regions, so please contact your local group to discuss how to help.
