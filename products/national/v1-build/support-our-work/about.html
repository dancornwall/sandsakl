About Sands

Sands New Zealand is a network of parent-run, non-profit groups supporting families across Aotearoa who have experienced the death of a baby — at any gestation, and under any circumstance. All our volunteers are also bereaved parents.

Sands offers empathy and understanding. We are not counsellors, but we offer an opportunity to share experiences, talk and listen. Local groups and the national network allow for recognition, acceptance and expression of our babies and our grief.

Parents and their families who experience the death of a baby — in pregnancy, at birth, as a newborn, and those who may be subsequently pregnant — are welcome to get in touch.

About the organisation

Sands New Zealand is voluntary, run by bereaved parents and families. We are an incorporated society and a registered charitable entity. Local groups run with a high degree of autonomy and reflect their communities, while sharing aims, resources and national programmes.

Meet the NZ Board

Our aims

  • To offer support to all who are affected by the death of a baby.
  • To provide parents, families and whānau with information to assist them in making informed decisions about the death of their baby.
  • To provide information and education to health and caring professionals about the experience of pregnancy, baby and infant loss.
  • To raise public awareness of pregnancy, baby and infant loss.

Our philosophical statement

Pregnancy, baby and infant loss often has a significant and lifelong impact on the lives of parents, families and whānau. Support through recognition, acceptance and acknowledgement of their diverse experience of loss can be vital to future wellbeing. Partnerships with health practitioners, community groups, Government agencies and the wider community are crucial to raising awareness of the experience and impact of pregnancy, baby and infant loss. The diversity of individuals must be acknowledged and respected. Sands New Zealand recognises and adheres to Te Tiriti o Waitangi.

How we're funded

The Ministry of Health funds support packs on a national basis. We receive no other government funding — everything else runs on grants and donations. We are registered with the Charities Commission (number tbc).
