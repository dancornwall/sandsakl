About Sands

Sands New Zealand is a parent-led network supporting parents and whānau across Aotearoa following the death of a baby during pregnancy, at birth, as a newborn or in infancy.

We offer compassionate, non-judgemental support regardless of the circumstances, the stage of pregnancy or how a baby died. Support remains available no matter how much time has passed, including during a pregnancy after loss.

The bulk of our volunteers are bereaved parents and whānau who have an understanding of the depth and complexity of baby loss through personal experience.

What we offer

Sands provides opportunities to connect, talk, listen and share experiences with people who understand. Depending on the local group, support may include meetings, phone calls, messages, online communities, practical information, memory-making resources and one-to-one support.

We are not trained counsellors and do not provide professional or medical advice. We offer empathy, understanding and a place where babies and grief can be openly acknowledged.

About our organisation

Sands New Zealand is a voluntary, non-profit organisation led by bereaved parents and whānau. We are an incorporated society and registered charitable entity.

Our local groups have a high degree of autonomy, allowing them to respond to the needs of their communities. The groups share the values, aims, resources and national programmes of Sands New Zealand, while the support available may differ between regions.

Meet the Sands New Zealand Board

Our aims

Sands New Zealand aims to:

  • support everyone affected by the death of a baby
  • provide parents and whānau with practical information to support informed choices
  • help healthcare and other caring professionals understand the experience and impact of pregnancy, baby and infant loss
  • raise public awareness of pregnancy, baby and infant loss
  • strengthen connections with organisations and communities working alongside bereaved whānau

What we believe

The death of a baby can have a profound and lasting impact on parents and whānau. Recognition, acknowledgement and compassionate support are important to their wellbeing.

Every experience of grief is different. We respect the diversity of the people and communities we support, including their cultures, beliefs, identities, circumstances and ways of grieving.

Partnerships with health practitioners, community organisations, government agencies and the wider community are vital to improving understanding and support for bereaved whānau.

Sands New Zealand recognises and honours Te Tiriti o Waitangi.

How we are funded

Sands is powered by volunteers and supported through grants, fundraising and donations. Some national bereavement resources are supported through the public health system, but Sands receives no operational government funding.

Donations help us provide support, information, volunteer training, memory-making resources and awareness activities throughout Aotearoa.

Sands New Zealand is a registered charitable entity.
