Volunteer training

Sands New Zealand provides training for members and local groups — time to learn, share practice, and look after the people who support bereaved families.

This is for Sands volunteers and group people, not the free student training offered with health education partners. For professional education, see For health professionals.

What training covers

  • Workshops and seminars with invited speakers
  • Time for area groups to share what they do
  • In conference years, sessions often sit alongside the national conference
  • In other years, a dedicated training weekend in a region of Aotearoa

Who should come

New groups are expected to attend training within two years of starting. Established groups are encouraged to send at least one member regularly. Confirm against current member guidelines.

Next training

Dates, venue and how to register will be published when the next training is confirmed. Check What's On for anything scheduled, or ask the board via secretary@sands.org.nz.
