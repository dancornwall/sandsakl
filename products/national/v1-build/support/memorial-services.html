Free memorial services

Some organisations and craftspeople offer free or donated items to families after a baby dies. Availability and terms change — please check each provider directly. This is not a paid endorsement list.

Links and offers need periodic verification. Commercial jewellery retail removed from the old national list by design.

Glover Memorials

Small memorial plaques offered free to Sands families (see their Community Care information). Lower Hutt.

glovermemorials.co.nz

Huggable Hearts

Fabric hearts made to a baby's birth weight — a free service for grieving families.

huggablehearts.co.nz

Fidds Workshop

Wooden ashes boxes offered free to parents (conditions and postage as stated by the maker).

fiddsworkshop.co.nz

Heartfelt

Volunteer professional photographers who gift photographs to families after stillbirth, premature birth or neonatal death — ask hospital staff or visit their site.

heartfelt.org.au

For ideas about creating memories yourself, see Memory making.
