Online support meetings

Sands New Zealand facilitates regular online support meetings through Zoom, allowing bereaved parents and whānau throughout Aotearoa to connect from their own homes.

You are welcome to share your experiences, talk about your baby or simply listen. There is no pressure to speak.

Meetings are generally held monthly, with a break or changes to the schedule during late December and early to mid-January. All meeting times are New Zealand time.

Empty Arms support meeting

For bereaved parents who have no living children.

Usual time: The second Wednesday of each month at 8pm

Holiday break: No meeting in January

Open support meeting

For all bereaved parents and whānau, regardless of when or how their baby died.

Usual time: The last Thursday of each month at 8pm

Holiday break: No meeting in December

Pregnancy After Loss support meeting

For parents who are pregnant following the death of a baby. These meetings also welcome parents who have recently welcomed a baby after loss and are navigating the first three to four months of parenting.

Usual time: The third Wednesday of each month at 7.30pm

Holiday schedule: Dates may change during the late December and January holiday period

Rainbow Whānau Baby Loss Support

For bereaved parents and whānau from LGBTQIA+ communities. This is a welcoming space to connect with others who understand the experiences of rainbow whānau following pregnancy, baby or infant loss.

Usual time: The last Wednesday of each month at 7pm

Holiday break: No meeting in December

Register for a meeting

Registration is required so we can send you the private Zoom link.

Email secretary@sands.org.nz and include:

  • your name
  • your email address
  • a contact phone number
  • your town, city or region in Aotearoa
  • the meeting you would like to attend

We will reply with confirmation and the Zoom details for the meeting.
