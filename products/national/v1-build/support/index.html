Support

There are lots of ways Sands can help — from finding a local group, to reading something on your own, to talking with a volunteer, to sitting with other bereaved parents. Start wherever feels right. Nothing here asks more of you than you have to give today.

Find a local group

Sands groups across Aotearoa offer phone support, email, and meetings — whatever your local group can sustain. Start with a group near you; if there isn't one, online groups are still open to you.

Browse groups by region

Talk to us

Sands volunteers offer phone and email support to bereaved parents. Local groups hold the everyday contacts; the national board can help if you're not sure where to start. Our volunteers are bereaved parents too — not counsellors. We listen, and we share.

More about contacting us

If you need to talk to someone right now — beyond Sands

These are free, 24-hour lines. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 111.

  • Healthline 0800 611 116
  • Lifeline 0800 543 354
  • Crisis Team, Auckland City (CATT) 0800 800 717
  • Plunketline 0800 933 922
  • Relationship Services 0800 735 283

Support group meetings

In person, with a local group

Many groups hold regular meetings — a safe, non-judgmental space to talk (if you wish) about your baby who has died, alongside others who understand. You needn't say anything at all. Patterns vary by group: day, venue, and how often.

Find your local group's contact and meeting pattern

Dated instances (this month's meeting, this year's remembrance) live on What's On when published nationally or by groups.

Online, any time

Sands New Zealand runs several closed Facebook groups — a way to connect with other grieving parents across the country, and to ask questions any time. You request to join, answer a few questions, and agree to the group guidelines, which keep them safe and respectful.

Remembrance & events

Through the year Sands groups and the national network hold events to remember our babies — for any gestation, and any time since your loss. These include Baby Loss Awareness Week, Light Up Aotearoa, the Global Wave of Light, and the biennial national conference.

See this year's dates and how to take part on What's On

Other support

When you're ready to look further afield, these organisations may help too:

  • Miscarriage Support — support after miscarriage (regional availability varies).
  • Miscarriage Matters — information and community.
  • Skylight Trust — grief support for all ages. skylight.org.nz

Whetūrangitia

Whetūrangitia is a government resource that brings together parents' rights and practical, legal, financial, burial and coronial information in one place, developed jointly across six agencies. The name speaks of stars returning to the sky to join the ancestors.
