Support resources

These resources from Sands New Zealand include free downloadable pamphlets, information about support packs and the Certificate of Life.

What's in a support pack

A support pack contains information pamphlets, inkless kits and special memory booklets. These items can be an important part of the memory-building process, as there is often so little time available to spend with our babies.

Packs are provided through hospitals and midwives in many regions (Ministry of Health–funded). Local groups and the national network can also help families and LMCs obtain packs. Contact us or your local group if you would like a pack or more information.

Certificate of Life

Every pēpi | baby, no matter how briefly they were with us, deserves to be recognised. The Certificate of Life is a keepsake for whānau who have experienced pregnancy loss before 20 weeks — a time when no formal birth or death registration is required by law, but when grief is no less real.

What it is

A personal document that acknowledges your baby's existence. It does not replace any legal document. Many whānau find that having something tangible — with a name, dates, and words that recognise their baby — brings a measure of comfort.

All parents are welcome to receive a certificate; the A4 size has space for a photo or hand and foot prints. Certificates are available through hospitals and Sands groups, and may be ordered via the national network.

How to get one

  • Ask your midwife, hospital or local Sands group
