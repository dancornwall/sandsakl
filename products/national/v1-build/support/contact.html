Contact us

Whenever you're ready, we're here. Getting in touch doesn't commit you to anything — you can ask a question, or just say hello. Whoever you reach is a bereaved parent too.

Local support (usually best first)

Most day-to-day phone and meeting support is through your local Sands group.

If you can't face making the first call yourself, a family member or friend is very welcome to get in touch on your behalf.

If you'd rather write than talk, email is always open.

What contact with Sands is — and isn't

We offer peer support: understanding from people who have experienced the death of a baby themselves. We are not counsellors, and we're not a crisis service. What we can offer is the chance to share, to talk, and to be heard by someone who truly understands.

If you need urgent help right now, the Support page lists free 24-hour crisis lines. See those numbers.
