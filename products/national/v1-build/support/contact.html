Contact us

Wherever you are in Aotearoa, you are welcome to contact Sands. Getting in touch does not commit you to attending a meeting or sharing more than feels comfortable.

Sands is parent-led, and many of our volunteers have personally experienced the death of a baby.

Find support in your region

Our Sands groups and contacts offer support in communities across Aotearoa. Because each group is volunteer-led, the support available varies between regions and may include meetings, phone calls, email or messages.

Find a Sands group near you

If there is no active group in your region, or local support does not suit your needs, you can access our online support or contact Sands New Zealand directly.

If making the first contact feels too difficult, a partner, family member, friend or healthcare professional can get in touch on your behalf with your permission.

You can email us at any time, but please remember that Sands is run by volunteers and replies may not be immediate.

What Sands support is

Sands offers peer support, understanding and connection with people who know something of the grief that follows the death of a baby. We offer a place to talk, share your baby’s story or simply be heard without judgement.

Sands does not provide counselling, medical advice or crisis support, and our messages are not monitored continuously.

If you need urgent help

If you need immediate emotional or mental health support, our Support page lists free services available throughout Aotearoa, 24 hours a day.

Find urgent support

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 111.
