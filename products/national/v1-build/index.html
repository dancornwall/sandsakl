Expecting or welcoming a baby is often a time of hope, dreams and plans for the future. When a baby dies, those hopes can feel shattered and the future a whānau imagined is changed forever.

Every baby matters, and every experience of loss deserves acknowledgement. Sands is a parent-led network supporting bereaved parents and whānau across Aotearoa, regardless of their circumstances, the stage of pregnancy or how their baby died.

Grief is deeply personal. There is no right way to grieve and no set timetable to follow. What can make a difference is having support and connection with people who understand.

This is why Sands exists: to offer compassion, comfort and peer support, so that bereaved whānau do not have to face the death of a baby alone. We want every whānau in Aotearoa to be able to find support, wherever they live.
