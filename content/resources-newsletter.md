## Expressions Magazine

Expressions is a quarterly magazine produced by Sands Auckland. This provides an alternative forum for sharing stories, poems, etc. Expressions may also contain information on our Sands group, training events, book reviews, yearly reports, committee meetings and conference write-ups. All Sands members are invited to contribute to our magazine – maybe a favourite poem, a letter to your baby, or a review of a book you have read.

If you would like to receive a copy, please [contact us](https://www.sandsauckland.org.nz/contact/) and we'll send you a PDF or post you the latest edition.

To view our latest PDF edition of Expressions, please click [here](https://www.sandsauckland.org.nz/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Sands-NL-Winter-26-v2-Web.pdf).
