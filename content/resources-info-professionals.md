## Information for Health Professionals

We promote self-care for health and caring professionals and are available by phone or email to offer information at any time during your care of a family (e.g. diagnosis, pregnancy, death, subsequent pregnancy, etc.)

PDF copies of all of the pamphlets that are provided in the Sands support pack (provided to hospitals) can be found at: [www.sands.org.nz/help-pamphlets.html](http://www.sands.org.nz/help-pamphlets.html)

### Training Days

Volunteers from Sands Auckland Central are also available to give talks for health professionals about Sands and our personal experiences. For more information on our training days, please click [here](https://www.sandsauckland.org.nz/events/training-days/).
