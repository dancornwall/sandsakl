Library & books

Reading can be a quiet companion. Below are some New Zealand books you can buy, and a large selection from Auckland Libraries you can borrow — organised so you can find what fits your loss.

In this page
  • New Zealand books
  • Auckland Libraries by loss type

New Zealand books

What's Happened to Baby?

By Skylight, Sands and SIDS. A sensitively written, illustrated picture book for children 3–7 who have had a baby die in their family, with notes for adults.

Buy from Skylight

A Tribute to Tabitha-Rose

Edited by Vicki Culling. Twenty-four stories of pregnancy, baby and infant loss in NZ. $20 per copy plus $5 postage, through Sands Wellington-Hutt Valley.

Order by email

Baby Gone

By Jenny Douché. Forty-five true New Zealand stories of infertility, miscarriage, stillbirth and infant loss, written from the heart by those affected.

babygone.com

Holding On & Letting Go

Compiled by Vicki Culling. Stories from parents who faced an unexpected diagnosis in pregnancy and made a decision — to hold on, or to let go.

holgbook.com

Auckland Libraries, by loss type

Auckland Libraries have a range of titles to borrow, in print and ebook. Below is a selection by topic. To see everything available, search their catalogue (e.g. “baby loss”, “miscarriage”, “stillbirth”, “grief”). Visit Auckland Libraries.

Baby loss

  • Life After Baby Loss: A Guide to Pregnancy and Infant Loss and Subsequent Pregnancy in New Zealand — Nicola Miller-Clendon (print)
  • Empty Cradle, Broken Heart: Surviving the Death of Your Baby — Deborah Davis (print & ebook)
  • Saying Goodbye: A Personal Story of Baby Loss — Zoe Clark-Coates (print)
  • Life After Baby Loss: A Companion and Guide for Parents — Nicola Gaskin (print & ebook)
  • Beyond Words: Grieving When Your Child Has Died — Andrew Thompson and Tricia Irving Hendry (print)

Stillbirth

  • Stillbirth, Yet Still Born: Grieving and Honoring Your Precious Baby — Deborah Davis (ebook)
  • Still Life: Hidden Stories of Stillbirth and Forbidden Grief — Lois Tonkin (print)
  • Changed: Living With Stillbirth — Liza Jankowski (print & ebook)
  • Hope and Healing After Stillbirth and New Baby Loss — Kevin Gournay and Dr Brenda Ashcroft (print)
  • Healing Your Grieving Heart After Stillbirth — Alan D. Wolfelt, Raelynn Maloney (print)

Neonatal / newborn death

  • Ask Me His Name: Learning to Live and Laugh Again After the Loss of My Baby — Elle Wright (print & ebook)
  • Expecting Sunshine: A Journey of Grief, Healing, and Pregnancy After Loss — Alexis Marie Chute (print)
  • Hope and Healing After Stillbirth and New Baby Loss — Kevin Gournay and Dr Brenda Ashcroft (print)

Miscarriage

  • Healing Your Grieving Heart After Miscarriage — Alan D. Wolfelt (ebook)
  • The Brink of Being: Talking About Miscarriage — Julia Bueno (print)
  • Miscarriage: What Every Woman Needs to Know — Professor Lesley Regan (print)
  • Miscarriage Mom: The Unspoken Realities of Miscarriage and How to Cope — Kristy Parisi (print)

Stories from parents

  • A Tribute to Tabitha-Rose: Stories of Baby and Infant Loss in New Zealand — Vicki Culling (print)
  • Baby Gone: True New Zealand Stories of Infertility, Miscarriage, Stillbirth and Infant Loss — Jenny Douché (print)
  • Motherhood Missed: Stories of Loss and Living From Women Who Are Childless by Circumstance — Lois Tonkin (print & ebook)
  • Loss, Survive, Thrive: Bereaved Parents Share Their Stories of Healing and Hope — Meryl Hershey Beck (print)
  • Defining Moments: Coping With the Loss of a Child — Melanie Warner (print)
  • Through, Not Around: Stories of Infertility and Pregnancy Loss — Allison McDonald Ace, Ariel Ng Bourbonnais, Caroline Starr (print & ebook)

Subsequent pregnancy

  • Expecting Sunshine: A Journey of Grief, Healing, and Pregnancy After Loss — Alexis Marie Chute (print)
  • Life After Baby Loss: A Guide to Pregnancy and Infant Loss and Subsequent Pregnancy in New Zealand — Nicola Miller-Clendon (print)

For children

  • What's Happened to Baby? — Members of SIDS (Wgtn), Sands (Wgtn) and Skylight; illustrated by Ali Teo (print)
  • Ben's Flying Flowers — Inger Maier (print)
