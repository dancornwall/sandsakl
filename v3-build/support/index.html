Support

There are lots of ways Sands can help — from simply reading something on your own, to talking with a volunteer, to sitting with other bereaved parents at a meeting. Start wherever feels right. Nothing here asks more of you than you have to give today.

Talk to us

Sands volunteers offer phone and email support to bereaved parents. If you can't make the first call yourself, a family member or friend can get in touch and we'll contact you. Our volunteers are bereaved parents too — not counsellors. We listen, and we share.

Phone: 0508 SANDSA (726 372) — a callback line. Leave a message and a volunteer will return your call, usually within 24 hours.
Email: info@sandsauckland.org.nz

More about contacting us

If you need to talk to someone right now — beyond Sands

These are free, 24-hour lines. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 111.

  • Healthline 0800 611 116
  • Lifeline 0800 543 354
  • Crisis Team, Auckland City (CATT) 0800 800 717
  • Plunketline 0800 933 922
  • Relationship Services 0800 735 283

Support group meetings

In person, once a month

Monthly meetings are a safe, non-judgmental space to talk — if you wish — about your baby who has died, alongside others who understand. You needn't say anything at all. Meetings are facilitated by one of our support volunteers, and are for adults only (there are no childcare facilities).

When
First Thursday of the month, 7:15–8:45 pm (February–December; no January meeting)
Where
Garlick Room, Mount Albert Community & Leisure Centre, 773 New North Rd, Mt Albert — turn left at reception

See the next meeting date on What's On

Online, any time

Sands New Zealand runs several closed Facebook groups — a way to connect with other grieving parents across the country, and to ask questions any time. You request to join, answer a few questions, and agree to the group guidelines, which keep them safe and respectful.

  • Bereaved Whānau Chat
  • Bereaved Dads Chat
  • Bereaved Grandparents Chat
  • Bereaved Whānau Information Page

Expressions — our newsletter

Expressions is our quarterly newsletter, with parents' stories, remembrance, and news from the group. You can receive it by PDF or by post, and you're warmly invited to contribute.

A note on privacy: the online edition leaves out the remembrance page by design; the emailed and printed editions include it.

Remembrance & events

Through the year we hold events to remember our babies — for any gestation, and any time since your loss, whether that was last month or twenty years ago. These include Baby Loss Awareness Week, the Global Wave of Light, and our Annual Remembrance Day.

See this year's dates, venues and how to come along on What's On

Other support

When you're ready to look further afield, these organisations may help too:

  • Sands New Zealand — the national network. sands.org.nz
  • Miscarriage Support — Auckland-based support after miscarriage.
  • Miscarriage Matters — information and community.
  • Skylight Trust — grief support for all ages. skylight.org.nz

Whetūrangitia

Whetūrangitia is a government resource that brings together parents' rights and practical, legal, financial, burial and coronial information in one place, developed jointly across six agencies. The name speaks of stars returning to the sky to join the ancestors.
